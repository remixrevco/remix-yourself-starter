# Where this goes when you want more

This free repo is the system taken apart and run by hand. You get the five tools, the shared memory, and the guide that teaches you to use them. That is real, and it is yours to keep, fork, and bend.

When you want it to do more, here is the ladder. Each rung is a real step up, not a paywall on what you already have.

## The rungs

**Free (you are here).** Learn the fundamentals and run the five workflows yourself. The compose contract, the shared memory, the guide. No cost, no account.

**Assembled.** $249, or $399 with 12 months of Membership. The same parts, assembled and automated. It sets itself up by interviewing you, wires into your email, calendar, and docs, runs overnight and on your phone, protects your data, syncs across your machines, and ships with the conventions that let a personal system scale without falling over. This is the part most people never figure out alone. The year of refinement, packaged.

**The Membership.** The standing home that keeps your system improving every week. New components, explainer videos, office hours, a health check, and the two GTM courses (CoWork for GTM and Claude Code for GTM).

**Personal Setup.** Three hours of face time where Andy builds your system with you, tuned to your role. $1,495, with a year of Membership included.

**Team.** Scoped rollout for a group. Reach out to talk through it.
