# How to Stick Them Together

Each component works alone. Drop two or more in the same folder and they share one memory, so the whole thing compounds. This is the preview of the assembled system, the Kit.

## The big idea

You have five working components. Each one is useful on its own. But the real magic is what happens when you put them in the same folder.

They share one memory. Your identity, your voice, your people, your projects. So the day you plan knows what came out of yesterday's calls. The brief you pull walks in already knowing the person. The follow-up you draft sounds like you. Nothing is wired together by hand. They just find the same memory, because they all agree on where it lives.

## How it works (the whole trick)

Every component reads and writes two folders at the root of wherever it runs:

```
your-folder/
├── context/        who you are, how you write, what you're working on
├── people/         one file per person you deal with
└── <components>    drop as many as you want, they all read the two above
```

That is the entire contract. No install, no config, no glue code. Put two components in `your-folder` and they share `context/` and `people/` automatically.

## Start with one, add as you go

1. **Pick one component.** Today-Planning is the fastest wow. Drop it in a folder, run it, feel it work.
2. **Add a second.** Drop Transcription Intake in the same folder. Now your planning knows what your meetings decided.
3. **Keep going.** Add the Writer so everything comes out in your voice. Add the Researcher so your meetings prep themselves. Each one you add makes the others better.

You do not have to plan the whole system. You build it one component at a time, and it compounds on its own.

## The rules that keep them from fighting

You do not need to know these to use it, but here is why it just works:

- **Read freely, write narrowly.** Every component can read your memory. Each only writes to its own corner of it.
- **Append, never clobber.** Components add to your files, dated. They never rewrite what you wrote.
- **Self-seed.** If `context/` or `people/` does not exist yet, the first component you run creates it. You never hit a wall.

The full shape, schemas, and read/write rules live in [`COMPOSE-CONTRACT.md`](./COMPOSE-CONTRACT.md).
