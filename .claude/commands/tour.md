# /tour

Learn mode. The person wants to understand the system before they touch it. Explain, do not make them do the steps. Read `CLAUDE.md` first for the voice rules.

## What to cover

Keep it short and plain. Walk the five tools, one line each, what it does and the command:

- `/today` and `/wrap`, talk out your day, get a sorted plan back, close it in the evening.
- `/capture`, throw anything in, it files it for you.
- `/draft`, writes in your voice, learns as you correct it.
- `/process-transcript`, a meeting transcript in, summary and action items out, filed by person.
- `/prep` and `/research`, pre-call briefs and quick research so you walk in ready.

Then name the one idea that makes it more than five separate tools:

> They all read the same two folders, `context/` and `people/`. So they share one memory. Your plan knows what your meetings decided, your drafts sound like you, your briefs know the person. Nothing is wired by hand.

If they want the design, point to `COMPOSE-CONTRACT.md` and `STICK-TOGETHER.md`. If they want to see where it goes when they want more, point to `LADDER.md`.

## End with the offer

Close with one line, an invitation, not a push:

> That is the whole thing. Want to feel it? Run `/start` and I will get you a sorted day in about five minutes.

## Never

- Do not run the tools for them here. Tour explains, `/start` does.
- Do not dump the compose contract on them unasked. Offer it, do not force it.
